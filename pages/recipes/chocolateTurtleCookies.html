Chocolate Turtle Cookies

Ingredients


  • 1 cup unsalted butter, softened
  • 1 cup brown sugar, packed
  • 1/2 cup granulated sugar
  • 2 large eggs
  • 2 tsp vanilla extract
  • 2 cups all-purpose flour
  • 1/2 cup cocoa powder
  • 1 tsp baking soda
  • 1/2 tsp salt

    • Mix-Ins

  • 1 cup chopped pecans
  • 1 cup semi-sweet chocolate chips
  • 20–24 soft caramel squares (or caramel bits)
  • Flaky sea salt (optional)

Instructions


  1. Preheat the oven: Set your oven to 350°F (175°C) and line a baking sheet with parchment paper.
  2. Cream the butter and sugars: In a large bowl, beat the softened butter, brown sugar, and granulated sugar until light and fluffy.
  3. Add eggs and vanilla: Mix in the eggs one at a time, then add the vanilla extract.
  4. Combine dry ingredients: In a separate bowl, whisk together the flour, cocoa powder, baking soda, and salt.
  5. Make the dough: Add the dry ingredients to the wet mixture and stir until a thick dough forms.
  6. Fold in mix-ins: Stir in the chocolate chips and chopped pecans.
  7. Shape the cookies: Scoop about 2 tablespoons of dough, press a caramel square into the center, and wrap the dough around it.
  8. Bake: Place cookies on the baking sheet and bake for 10–12 minutes, until edges are set but centers remain soft.
  9. Finish: While warm, sprinkle a pinch of flaky sea salt on top (optional but delicious).
  10. Cool and enjoy: Let cookies cool for 10 minutes so the caramel sets slightly before serving.
