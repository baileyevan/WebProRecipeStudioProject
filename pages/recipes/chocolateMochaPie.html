Chocolate Mocha Pie

Ingredients

  • 1 1/4 cups chocolate cookie crumbs
  • 1/4 cup unsalted butter, melted
  • 1 cup semi‑sweet chocolate chips
  • 1/4 cup strong brewed coffee (cooled)
  • 1 cup heavy whipping cream
  • 8 oz cream cheese, softened
  • 1/2 cup granulated sugar
  • 1 tsp vanilla extract
  • Whipped cream (for topping)
  • Chocolate shavings (optional)

Instructions

  1. Prepare the crust: In a bowl, mix the chocolate cookie crumbs and melted butter until fully combined. Press the mixture firmly into a pie dish to form an even crust. Chill for 15 minutes.
  2. Melt the chocolate: In a microwave‑safe bowl, melt the chocolate chips in 20‑second intervals, stirring until smooth. Let cool slightly.
  3. Make the filling: In a large bowl, beat the cream cheese, sugar, and vanilla until smooth. Add the cooled coffee and melted chocolate, mixing until fully incorporated.
  4. Whip the cream: In a separate bowl, whip the heavy cream until stiff peaks form. Gently fold the whipped cream into the chocolate mixture.
  5. Assemble: Pour the filling into the chilled crust and smooth the top.
  6. Chill: Refrigerate the pie for at least 4 hours or until set.
  7. Serve: Top with whipped cream and chocolate shavings before serving. Enjoy!
