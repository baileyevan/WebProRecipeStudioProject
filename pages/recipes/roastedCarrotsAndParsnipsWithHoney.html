Roasted Carrots and Parsnips with Honey

Ingredients

  • 4 large carrots, peeled and cut into sticks
  • 4 parsnips, peeled and cut into sticks
  • 2 tbsp olive oil
  • 2 tbsp honey
  • 1 tbsp fresh lemon juice
  • 1 tsp dried thyme (or 1 tbsp fresh)
  • Salt and black pepper, to taste
  • Fresh parsley, chopped (for garnish)

Instructions

  1. Preheat the oven: Set your oven to 400°F (200°C). Line a baking sheet with parchment paper.
  2. Prepare the vegetables: Peel and slice the carrots and parsnips into even-sized sticks for uniform roasting.
  3. Season: In a large bowl, toss the vegetables with olive oil, honey, lemon juice, thyme, salt, and pepper until evenly coated.
  4. Spread on the pan: Arrange the vegetables in a single layer on the baking sheet.
  5. Roast: Bake for 25–30 minutes, flipping halfway through, until the edges are caramelized and the vegetables are tender.
  6. Finish: Remove from the oven and sprinkle with fresh parsley.
  7. Serve: Enjoy warm as a side dish with chicken, fish, or holiday meals.
