Cream of Asparagus Soup

Ingredients

  • 2 tbsp unsalted butter
  • 1 tbsp olive oil
  • 1 small onion, finely chopped
  • 2 cloves garlic, minced
  • 1 1/2 lbs fresh asparagus, trimmed and cut into 1-inch pieces
  • 3 cups vegetable or chicken broth
  • 1 cup heavy cream (or half-and-half)
  • 1 medium potato, peeled and diced (for extra creaminess, optional)
  • Salt and black pepper, to taste
  • 1–2 tbsp lemon juice, to taste
  • Sour cream or crème fraîche (for serving, optional)
  • Chopped fresh chives or parsley (for garnish)

Instructions

  1. Sauté aromatics: In a large pot, heat the butter and olive oil over medium heat. Add the chopped onion and cook for 4–5 minutes until softened. Stir in the garlic and cook for 30 seconds until fragrant.
  2. Add asparagus and potato: Add the asparagus pieces and diced potato (if using). Cook for 3–4 minutes, stirring occasionally.
  3. Add broth and simmer: Pour in the broth and bring to a gentle boil. Reduce heat and simmer for 15–20 minutes, or until the asparagus and potato are very tender.
  4. Blend the soup: Remove from heat. Using an immersion blender, blend the soup until smooth. (Or carefully transfer to a blender in batches.)
  5. Add cream: Return the soup to low heat. Stir in the heavy cream and warm gently—do not boil. Season with salt, black pepper, and lemon juice to taste.
  6. Serve: Ladle into bowls and top with a swirl of sour cream or crème fraîche and a sprinkle of fresh chives or parsley. Serve warm.
