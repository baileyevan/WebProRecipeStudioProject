Churro Cookies

Ingredients


    Cinnamon Sugar Coating

  • ½ cup granulated sugar
  • 1 tablespoon ground cinnamon

    • Cookie Dough

  • 2 cups all-purpose flour
  • 1 teaspoon baking powder
  • ½ teaspoon salt
  • ¾ cup unsalted butter
  • ¾ cup granulated sugar
  • 1 egg
  • 1 teaspoon vanilla extract
  • 1 teaspoon ground cinnamon

Instructions


    Cinnamon Sugar Coating

  1. Mix coating: Combine granulated sugar and ground cinnamon in a small bowl.
  2. Set aside.

    3. Cookie Dough

  3. Preheat oven: Set oven to 350°F (175°C) and line baking sheets with parchment paper.
  4. Combine dry ingredients: In a bowl whisk together flour, baking powder, cinnamon, and salt.
  5. Cream butter and sugars: Beat butter and sugar until fluffy.
  6. Add egg and vanilla.
  7. Combine mixtures: Slowly add dry ingredients and mix until dough forms.
  8. Shape cookies: Roll dough into balls and coat in cinnamon sugar mixture.
  9. Bake: Bake cookies for 10–12 minutes until lightly golden.
  10. Cool: Let cookies cool slightly before serving.
