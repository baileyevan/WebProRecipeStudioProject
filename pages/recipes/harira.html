Harira

Ingredients

  • 2 tbsp olive oil
  • 1 onion, finely chopped
  • 2 celery stalks, chopped
  • 2 carrots, diced
  • 3 cloves garlic, minced
  • 1 tsp ground turmeric
  • 1 tsp ground cumin
  • 1 tsp ground ginger
  • 1/2 tsp cinnamon
  • 1/2 tsp black pepper
  • Salt, to taste
  • 1 cup cooked chickpeas
  • 1/2 cup red lentils
  • 1 can (14 oz) crushed tomatoes
  • 6 cups vegetable or chicken broth
  • 2 tbsp flour mixed with 1/4 cup water (optional)
  • 1/4 cup fresh cilantro, chopped
  • 1/4 cup fresh parsley, chopped
  • Juice of 1/2 lemon

Instructions

  1. Sauté the vegetables: Heat olive oil in a large pot. Add onion, celery, carrots, and garlic. Cook for 5–7 minutes until softened.
  2. Add spices: Stir in turmeric, cumin, ginger, cinnamon, black pepper, and salt. Cook for 1 minute to release the aromas.
  3. Add legumes and tomatoes: Add chickpeas, red lentils, and crushed tomatoes. Stir to combine.
  4. Add broth and simmer: Pour in the broth and bring to a boil. Reduce heat and simmer for 30–35 minutes, until lentils are tender.
  5. Thicken (optional): Stir in the flour-water mixture and simmer for 5 more minutes until slightly thickened.
  6. Finish the soup: Add chopped cilantro, parsley, and lemon juice. Adjust seasoning to taste.
  7. Serve: Enjoy warm with crusty bread or dates — traditional Moroccan style.
