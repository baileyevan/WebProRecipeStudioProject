Buckeye Cookies

Ingredients


    Chocolate Cookie Dough

  • 1 cup unsalted butter, softened
  • ¾ cup granulated sugar
  • ¾ cup packed brown sugar
  • 2 large eggs
  • 2 teaspoons vanilla extract
  • 2 cups all-purpose flour
  • &frac23 cup unsweetened cocoa powder
  • 1 teaspoon baking soda
  • ½ teaspoon salt

    • Peanut Butter Filling

  • ¾ cup creamy peanut butter
  • 3 tablespoons unsalted butter, softened
  • 1 cup powdered sugar
  • ½ teaspoon vanilla extract

    • Chocolate Topping

  • 1 cup (170 g) semi-sweet chocolate chips
  • 1 teaspoon coconut oil or vegetable oil (optional, for smoother melting)

Instructions


    Peanut Butter Filling

  1. Prepare filling: Mix peanut butter, powdered sugar, and softened butter until smooth.
  2. Shape filling: Roll mixture into small balls and place in refrigerator for 20 minutes.

    3. Chocolate Cookie Dough

  3. Preheat oven: Set oven to 350°F (175°C).
  4. Combine dry ingredients: Whisk flour, cocoa powder, baking soda, and salt.
  5. Cream butter and sugars: Beat butter, brown sugar, and granulated sugar until fluffy.
  6. Add egg and vanilla.
  7. Combine mixtures: Add dry ingredients and mix until dough forms.
  8. Shape cookies: Flatten a scoop of dough and place peanut butter ball in center.
  9. Seal dough: Wrap dough around filling to fully enclose it.
  10. Bake: Bake for 10–12 minutes.
  11. Cool: Allow cookies to cool before serving.
