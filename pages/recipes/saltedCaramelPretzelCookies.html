Salted Caramel Pretzel Cookies

Ingredients


    Pretzel Mix

  • 1 cup crushed pretzels
  • ½ cup caramel bits
  • ½ teaspoon flaky sea salt

    • Cookie Dough

  • 2 cups all-purpose flour
  • 1 teaspoon baking soda
  • ¾ cup butter
  • ½ cup granulated sugar
  • ¾ cup brown sugar
  • 1 egg
  • 1 teaspoon vanilla extract

Instructions


    Pretzel Caramel Mix

  1. Prepare mix: In a small bowl combine crushed pretzels and caramel bits.
  2. Set aside: Keep mixture ready for folding into the dough later.

    3. Cookie Dough

  3. Preheat the oven: Set oven to 350°F (175°C) and line baking sheets with parchment paper.
  4. Combine dry ingredients: In a bowl whisk together flour and baking soda.
  5. Cream butter and sugars: In a large mixing bowl beat butter, brown sugar, and granulated sugar until fluffy.
  6. Add egg and vanilla: Mix until fully incorporated.
  7. Combine wet and dry mixtures: Gradually add flour mixture and mix until a dough forms.
  8. Fold ingredients together: Stir in pretzel and caramel mixture until evenly distributed.
  9. Shape cookies: Scoop medium portions of dough and place on prepared baking sheets.
  10. Add finishing salt: Sprinkle a small pinch of flaky sea salt on top of each cookie.
  11. Bake: Bake for 10–12 minutes until edges are lightly golden.
  12. Cool and enjoy: Let cookies rest for 5 minutes before transferring to a cooling rack.
