Cinnamon Roll Cookies

Ingredients


    Cinnamon Filling

  • 3 tablespoons unsalted butter, softened
  • ½ cup brown sugar
  • 1 tablespoon cinnamon

    • Cookie Dough

  • 2 cups all-purpose flour
  • 1 teaspoon baking powder
  • ½ teaspoon salt
  • ¾ cup butter
  • ¾ cup granulated sugar
  • 1 egg
  • 1 teaspoon vanilla extract

    • Cream Cheese Glaze

  • 4 oz cream cheese
  • 1 cup powdered sugar
  • 1 teaspoon vanilla
  • 2 tablespoons milk

Instructions


    Cinnamon Filling

  1. Prepare filling: Mix softened butter, brown sugar, and cinnamon until a spreadable paste forms.
  2. Set aside.

    3. Cookie Dough

  3. Prepare dough: In a bowl whisk flour, baking powder, and salt.
  4. Cream butter and sugar: Beat butter and sugar until fluffy.
  5. Add egg and vanilla.
  6. Combine mixtures: Add dry ingredients until dough forms.
  7. Roll dough: Roll dough into a rectangle on a lightly floured surface.
  8. Add filling: Spread cinnamon filling evenly across dough.
  9. Roll and chill: Roll dough into a log and refrigerate for 30 minutes.
  10. Slice cookies: Cut log into ½ inch rounds.
  11. Bake: Bake at 350°F (175°C) for 10–12 minutes.

    12. Cream Cheese Glaze

  12. Prepare glaze: Mix powdered sugar, cream cheese, vanilla, and milk until smooth.
  13. Drizzle over cooled cookies.
