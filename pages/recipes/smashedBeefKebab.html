Smashed Beef Kebab

Ingredients

  • 1 lb ground beef (80/20 recommended)
  • 1 small onion, grated or finely minced
  • 3 cloves garlic, minced
  • 1/4 cup fresh parsley, finely chopped
  • 1 tsp ground cumin
  • 1 tsp smoked paprika
  • 1/2 tsp ground coriander
  • 1/2 tsp black pepper
  • 1 tsp salt
  • 1 tbsp olive oil (for cooking)
  • Pita or flatbread
  • Sliced tomatoes
  • Sliced onions
  • Tzatziki or garlic sauce
  • Fresh herbs (parsley or mint)

Instructions

  1. Prepare the mixture: In a bowl, combine ground beef, grated onion, garlic, parsley, cumin, paprika, coriander, salt, and pepper. Mix gently until just combined.
  2. Shape the kebabs: Divide the mixture into 6–8 portions. Roll each into a ball, then flatten slightly into thick patties.
  3. Heat the pan: Add olive oil to a cast‑iron skillet or heavy pan over medium‑high heat.
  4. Smash and sear: Place a beef portion in the hot pan and press down firmly with a spatula to flatten. Cook 2–3 minutes per side until browned and cooked through.
  5. Repeat: Continue smashing and cooking the remaining kebabs.
  6. Assemble: Serve the kebabs in pita or flatbread with tomatoes, onions, herbs, and your favorite sauce.
  7. Enjoy: Serve warm for the best flavor and texture.
