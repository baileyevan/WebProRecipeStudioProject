FAQ

How do I find a recipe or section?

All recipes are separated by different categories after you nagivate to the "Browse All" button at the bottom of the home page.

Can servings be changed?

Each recipe can be adjusted. If you need to serve more or less people, just multiply the ingredient quanties accordingly. Keep in mind that preparation and cooking times may need to be adjusted.

Can I substitute ingredients?

Of course! Most recipes can easily be adjusted for dietary tweaks.
For diabetics and low sugar people, stevia and splenda can be substituted.
Vegans and vegetarians can use egg-replacements and dairy free milk/cream cheese/butter/etc.
Gluten-free people can use wheat-free flour (all-purpose or self-rising depending on the recipe) such as almond flour, potato flour, rice flour, and corn flour.

What does is mean to be vegan/vegatarian?

A vegan diet is one that is completely free of any animal products! So this means no meat, eggs, dairy, or seafood.
A vegetarian diet is one where you don't eat meat, but you (occasionally) eat other animal products such as dairy, eggs, etc.

What does gluten-free mean?

Gluten is a protein found in several grains, therefore gluten-free is anything without those particular grains. Wheat is the most common grain containing gluten and can be found in breads, pastas, cakes, cookies, cereals, etc. Gluten-free recipes are best for those who are either allergic or intolerant to gluten, or for those who suffer from celiac disease. However, even if you tolerate gluten just fine, it’s unfortunately been proven to cause inflammation in all of our bodies.

What does dairy-free mean?

Dairy-free means a recipe or diet that is completely free of any milk or milk products. The most common examples of milk products are yogurt, cheese, and butter. The reason why most people are dairy-free or enjoy dairy-free recipes is because they experience some sort of lactose intolerance. Lactose is a sugar found in all dairy products, and those who are lactose intolerant simply lack the proper enzyme in their gut to digest lactose. However, similar to gluten, dairy has been proven to cause inflammation in the body, whether you’re lactose intolerant or not.

How should food be stored and reheated?

Make sure all food is cooled down completely and free of steam before packing it and putting it in the freezer or fridge. Most food can be kept safely in the freezer for a couple weeks if stored in an airtight bag or container.

Why doesn't my food look the same as the picture?

Any number of small variations can happen due to ingredient brands, cooking equipment, or presentation. Focus more on the taste and texture rather than the exact appearance.
