Philly Fluff Cake

Ingredients

  • 1 cup unsalted butter, softened
  • 1 1/2 cups granulated sugar
  • 3 large eggs
  • 8 oz cream cheese, softened
  • 2 cups all-purpose flour
  • 1 tsp baking powder
  • 1/2 tsp salt
  • 1 tsp vanilla extract
  • 1/2 cup whole milk
  • Powdered sugar (for dusting)

Instructions

  1. Preheat the oven: Set your oven to 350°F (175°C). Grease and flour a bundt pan or loaf pan.
  2. Cream butter, cream cheese, and sugar: In a large bowl, beat the butter, cream cheese, and sugar together until light and fluffy.
  3. Add eggs: Mix in the eggs one at a time, beating well after each addition.
  4. Add vanilla: Stir in the vanilla extract.
  5. Mix dry ingredients: In a separate bowl, whisk together the flour, baking powder, and salt.
  6. Combine batter: Add the dry ingredients to the wet mixture, alternating with the milk, mixing until smooth.
  7. Fill the pan: Pour the batter into the prepared pan and smooth the top.
  8. Bake: Bake for 55–65 minutes, or until a toothpick inserted into the center comes out clean.
  9. Cool: Let the cake cool in the pan for 10 minutes, then transfer to a wire rack to cool completely.
  10. Finish: Dust with powdered sugar before serving.
