Instructions


  1. Step: instruction
  2. Preheat the oven: Set oven to 350°F (175°C) and prepare two baking sheets with parchment paper.
  3. Cream the butter and sugars: In a large mixing bowl, beat sugar, butter, egg, vanilla extract and salt until creamy.
  4. Combine dry ingredients: Add the flour, baking soda and matcha powder and knead all ingredients into a smooth cookie dough.
  5. Fold in mix-ins: Fold in the macadamia nuts and white chocolate chips using a spatula or cooking spoon.
  6. Shape and place the cookies: Use a medium cookie spoon to form the cookie dough balls, the amount of 1 heaped tablespoon. Place them on the parchment paper and leave at least 2 inches / 5cm between each cookie.
  7. Bake: Place cookies in the preheated oven for 12 - 15 minutes.
  8. Cool and enjoy: Let the cookies cool down for 3-5 minutes. Then transfer them on a cooling rack and let the green tea cookies rest for another 10 minutes.
