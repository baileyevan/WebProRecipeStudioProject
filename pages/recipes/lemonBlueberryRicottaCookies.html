Lemon Blueberry Ricotta Cookies

Ingredients


    Cookie Dough

  • 2 cups all-purpose flour
  • 1 teaspoon baking powder
  • ½ teaspoon salt
  • ½ cup butter
  • ¾ cup sugar
  • 1 cup ricotta cheese
  • 1 egg
  • 1 tablespoon lemon zest
  • 1 tablespoon lemon juice
  • 1 teaspoon vanilla
  • ¾ cup blueberries

    • Lemon Glaze

  • 1 cup powdered sugar
  • 2 tablespoons lemon juice

Instructions


    Cookie Dough

  1. Preheat the oven: Set oven to 350°F (175°C) and prepare baking sheets with parchment paper.
  2. Combine dry ingredients: In a bowl whisk together flour, baking powder, and salt.
  3. Cream butter and sugar: In a large bowl beat butter and sugar until light and fluffy.
  4. Add ricotta and egg: Mix ricotta cheese and egg into the butter mixture until smooth.
  5. Add flavoring: Stir in lemon zest, lemon juice, and vanilla extract.
  6. Combine mixtures: Gradually add dry ingredients and mix until a soft dough forms.
  7. Fold in blueberries: Gently stir blueberries into the dough without crushing them.
  8. Shape cookies: Scoop dough onto prepared baking sheets leaving space between each cookie.
  9. Bake: Bake for 12–14 minutes until the bottoms are lightly golden.

    10. Lemon Glaze

  10. Prepare glaze: Whisk powdered sugar and lemon juice until smooth.
  11. Glaze cookies: Once cookies are fully cooled, drizzle or spoon glaze over the tops.
  12. Set glaze: Allow glaze to set for about 10 minutes before serving.
