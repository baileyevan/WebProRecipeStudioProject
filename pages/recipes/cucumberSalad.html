Cucumber Salad

Ingredients

  • 2 large cucumbers, thinly sliced
  • 1/2 red onion, thinly sliced
  • 1/4 cup fresh dill, chopped
  • 1/4 cup sour cream or Greek yogurt
  • 1 tbsp olive oil
  • 1 tbsp white vinegar or lemon juice
  • 1 tsp sugar (optional)
  • Salt and black pepper, to taste

Instructions

  1. Prepare the vegetables: Thinly slice the cucumbers and red onion. Add them to a large bowl.
  2. Make the dressing: In a small bowl, whisk together sour cream (or Greek yogurt), olive oil, vinegar or lemon juice, sugar, salt, and pepper.
  3. Combine: Pour the dressing over the cucumbers and onions. Add the chopped dill and toss gently to coat.
  4. Chill: Refrigerate for at least 20–30 minutes to let the flavors blend.
  5. Serve: Enjoy cold as a refreshing side dish.
