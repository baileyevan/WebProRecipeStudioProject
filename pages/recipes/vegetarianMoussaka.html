Vegetarian Moussaka

Ingredients

  • 2 large eggplants, sliced into 1/2-inch rounds
  • 2 medium potatoes, thinly sliced
  • 1 large zucchini, sliced
  • 2 tbsp olive oil (for brushing)
  • 1 tbsp olive oil
  • 1 onion, finely chopped
  • 3 cloves garlic, minced
  • 1 cup cooked brown or green lentils
  • 1 can (14 oz) crushed tomatoes
  • 1 tsp dried oregano
  • 1 tsp dried basil
  • 1/2 tsp cinnamon
  • Salt and pepper, to taste
  • 3 tbsp butter
  • 3 tbsp all-purpose flour
  • 2 cups milk
  • 1/4 cup grated Parmesan or feta
  • 1/4 tsp nutmeg
  • Salt and pepper, to taste

Instructions

  1. Prepare the vegetables: Preheat oven to 400°F (200°C). Brush eggplant, zucchini, and potato slices with olive oil. Arrange on baking sheets and roast for 20–25 minutes until tender and lightly golden.
  2. Make the lentil filling: In a pan, heat olive oil and sauté onion until soft. Add garlic and cook for 1 minute. Stir in lentils, crushed tomatoes, oregano, basil, cinnamon, salt, and pepper. Simmer for 10 minutes.
  3. Prepare the béchamel: Melt butter in a saucepan. Whisk in flour and cook for 1 minute. Slowly add milk while whisking until thickened. Stir in Parmesan (or feta), nutmeg, salt, and pepper. Remove from heat.
  4. Assemble the moussaka: In a baking dish, layer potatoes first, then eggplant, then zucchini. Spread the lentil filling evenly over the vegetables.
  5. Add the béchamel: Pour the béchamel sauce over the top and smooth it out.
  6. Bake: Reduce oven temperature to 375°F (190°C). Bake for 30–35 minutes until the top is golden and bubbling.
  7. Cool and serve: Let the moussaka rest for 10–15 minutes before slicing. Enjoy warm.
