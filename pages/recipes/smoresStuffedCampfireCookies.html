S’mores Stuffed Campfire Cookies

Ingredients


    S’mores Filling

  • ½ cup mini marshmallows
  • ½ cup chocolate chunks
  • ½½ cup crushed graham crackers

    • Cookie Dough

  • 2 ¼ cups all-purpose flour
  • 1 teaspoon baking soda
  • ¾ cup unsalted butter, softened
  • ½ cup granulated sugar
  • ¾ cup brown sugar
  • 1 egg
  • 1 teaspoon vanilla extract
  • ½ cup chocolate chips

Instructions


    S’mores Filling

  1. Prepare filling mixture: In a small bowl combine mini marshmallows, chocolate chunks, and crushed graham crackers.
  2. Set aside: Place the filling mixture in the refrigerator while preparing the cookie dough. This helps prevent the marshmallows from melting too quickly during baking.

    3. Cookie Dough

  3. Preheat the oven: Set oven to 350°F (175°C) and line a baking sheet with parchment paper.
  4. Combine dry ingredients: In a bowl whisk together flour and baking soda.
  5. Cream butter and sugars: In a large bowl beat butter, granulated sugar, and brown sugar until light and fluffy.
  6. Add wet ingredients: Mix in egg and vanilla extract until fully combined.
  7. Combine mixtures: Gradually add the dry ingredients into the wet ingredients and mix until a smooth dough forms.
  8. Shape cookies: Scoop dough, flatten slightly, place 1 tablespoon of the filling in the center, and wrap dough around it to seal.
  9. Arrange cookies: Place dough balls on prepared baking sheet leaving 2 inches between each cookie.
  10. Bake: Bake for 10–12 minutes until edges are golden.
  11. Cool and enjoy: Allow cookies to cool for 5 minutes before transferring to a wire rack.
