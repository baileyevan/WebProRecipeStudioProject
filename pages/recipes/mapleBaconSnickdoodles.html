Maple Bacon Snickerdoodles

Ingredients


    Candied Bacon

  • 4 slices thick cut bacon
  • Maple Syrup, to coat bacon
  • ½ cup light or dark brown sugar

    • Maple Cinnamon Sugar

  • ¼ cup maple sugar
  • 2 tablespoons granualted sugar
  • 2 teaspoons ground cinnamon

    • Snickerdoodle Dough

  • 2 ¼ cups all-purpose baking flour
  • 2 teaspoons baking soda
  • 1 ½ teaspoons ground cinnamon
  • 1 teaspoon cream of tartar
  • ¾ cup unsalted butter, room temp
  • ½ cups granulated sugar
  • ¼ cups light brown sugar
  • 1 egg, room temp
  • ¼ cup maple syrup
  • 1 teaspoon vanilla extract
  • 1 cup dark chocolate wafers, melted

Instructions


    Candied Bacon

  1. Preheat the oven: Set oven to 350°F (175°C) and prepare two baking sheets with parchment paper.

    2. Maple Cinnamon Sugar Coating

  2. Mix Coating: In a small bowl, whisk together maple sugar, granulated sugar and cinnamon.
  3. Set aside.

    4. Snickerdoodle Dough

  4. Combine dry ingredients: Add the flour, baking soda and cround cinnamon, and cream of tartar and knead all ingredients into a smooth cookie dough.
  5. Cream the butter and sugars: In a large mixing bowl, beat butter, sugar, brown sugar until fluffy.
  6. Finish beating: Add egg, maple syrup, and vanilla extract to creamed bowl. Beat until completely combined.
  7. Fold ingredients together : Slowly add dry ingredients to wet ingredients and mix on medium speed until completely combined.
    Add crumbled bacon and stir to combine. If including chocolate chips, stir them in now as well.
  8. Set dough: Place dough in the fridge to chill for at least 1 hour.
  9. Preheat the oven: Set oven to 375°F (195°C) and prepare a baking sheet with parchment paper.
  10. Shape and place the cookies: Measure out each ball of dough using a medium cookie scoop, and roll each in the cinnamon sugar mixture. Place at least a couple inches apart on prepared baking sheet and bake.
  11. Bake: Place cookies in the preheated oven for 8-10 minutes, or until the tops have cracked.
  12. Cool and enjoy: Let the cookies cool down for 3-5 minutes. Then transfer them on a cooling rack and let them rest for another 8-10 minutes.
  13. Optional: Drizzle with melted chocolate and let set. Serve.
