Pizza Margherita

Ingredients

  • 2 1/4 cups all-purpose flour
  • 1 cup warm water
  • 1 packet (2 1/4 tsp) active dry yeast
  • 1 tsp sugar
  • 1 tsp salt
  • 1 tbsp olive oil
  • 1/2 cup tomato sauce (San Marzano preferred)
  • 8 oz fresh mozzarella, sliced
  • Fresh basil leaves
  • 1–2 tbsp olive oil
  • Salt & pepper, to taste

Instructions

  1. Activate the yeast: In a bowl, mix warm water, sugar, and yeast. Let sit for 5–10 minutes until foamy.
  2. Make the dough: Add flour, salt, and olive oil. Mix until a dough forms, then knead for 5–7 minutes until smooth.
  3. Let it rise: Place dough in a lightly oiled bowl, cover, and let rise for 1 hour or until doubled in size.
  4. Preheat the oven: Heat your oven to 475°F (245°C). If using a pizza stone, preheat it as well.
  5. Shape the dough: Punch down the dough and stretch or roll it into a 12-inch circle.
  6. Add toppings: Spread tomato sauce over the dough. Add mozzarella slices evenly. Drizzle with olive oil and season lightly with salt and pepper.
  7. Bake: Transfer to a baking sheet or pizza stone and bake for 10–12 minutes, or until the crust is golden and cheese is bubbly.
  8. Finish: Top with fresh basil leaves right after baking.
  9. Serve: Slice and enjoy warm.
